About FoodHealth AI

An AI-powered food detection and health analysis system

Final Year Project — 2024
Project Overview

FoodHealth AI is a comprehensive web-based application designed to help individuals monitor their food intake, understand nutritional values, and receive personalized health recommendations. The system combines artificial intelligence, machine learning, and modern web technologies to deliver a complete dietary and health management platform.

The project was built as part of our final year Computer Science curriculum to demonstrate the practical application of AI and machine learning in healthcare and daily lifestyle management.

System Architecture
1
Input Layer

User uploads food image or searches by name. Profile data includes age, weight, height, and health goals.

2
Processing Layer

MobileNetV2 classifies food images. Rule-based + ML algorithms compute recommendations and disease risks.

3
Output Layer

Nutritional values, personalized diet plans, risk scores, and comprehensive health reports are displayed.
